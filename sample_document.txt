NORTHSTAR LEARNING - 2026 PROGRAM IMPACT REPORT

About this document
This fictional report was created as a safe demonstration document for the RAG Document Chatbot project. All organizations, people, programs, and figures below are invented.

Organization overview
Northstar Learning is a fictional nonprofit education organization that provides online professional-skills courses to adult learners. Its mission is to make practical career education accessible to people changing careers or returning to the workforce.

2026 participation
Northstar Learning enrolled 4,800 learners during 2026, an increase of 20 percent from 4,000 learners in 2025. Learners participated from 18 states. Sixty-two percent of learners completed at least one full course, compared with 55 percent in the previous year.

Course results
The Data Fundamentals course enrolled 1,450 learners and recorded a completion rate of 74 percent. The Digital Marketing course enrolled 1,200 learners and recorded a completion rate of 69 percent. The Project Management course enrolled 1,050 learners and recorded a completion rate of 78 percent. The Career Communication course enrolled 1,100 learners and recorded a completion rate of 66 percent.

The Project Management course had the highest completion rate at 78 percent. Learner surveys showed that its weekly practice exercises and short video lessons were especially helpful.

Learner outcomes
Six months after completing a course, 48 percent of surveyed learners reported receiving a promotion, finding a new job, or taking on expanded responsibilities. Eighty-six percent said they would recommend a Northstar Learning course to another learner. The organization cautions that these outcomes are self-reported and should not be interpreted as proof that a course directly caused an employment change.

Technology and support
During 2026, Northstar Learning introduced mobile-friendly lessons, automated progress reminders, and evening virtual office hours. Support requests received an initial response in an average of 9 hours, improving from 15 hours in 2025.

Plans for 2027
Northstar Learning plans to add introductory courses in artificial intelligence and cybersecurity. It will also pilot multilingual captions, expand weekend office hours, and create a mentorship program for 300 learners. The organization aims to increase the overall course completion rate from 62 percent to 68 percent.

Conclusion
Northstar Learning experienced growth in enrollment, course completion, and learner support during 2026. Its 2027 priorities are new technology-focused courses, improved accessibility, expanded support, and mentorship.
